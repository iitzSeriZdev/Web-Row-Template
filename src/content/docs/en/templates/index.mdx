Row-Template 1.2.0 ships fifteen designs. **Row is the default.** Each design is a complete, self-contained page; you can switch between them at any time without losing your branding.

<TemplateGallery />

## What every design has in common

A design changes the layout and the look — never the features, the data or the safety checks. All fifteen are built from the same runtime and translations, so in every one of them:

- **Status, traffic and expiry** come from your panel and refresh while the page is open.
- **Connect** offers one-tap import into the apps for the subscriber's platform, and **Copy** and **QR** work the same way.
- The **Configuration Explorer** lists every server on its own row, with search for long lists.
- **Five languages** — English, Persian, Arabic, Russian and Chinese — with full right-to-left layout.
- **Light and dark themes**, following the device or the subscriber's choice.
- Your **service name, support link and logo** appear where the design places its brand.
- The page makes **no third-party requests**.

Each design ships in the release with its own SHA-256 checksum, and `row-template verify` checks every installed design against it.

## About the previews

The previews on these pages are the real design files, rendered by Row-Template's own fixture renderer from the project's placeholder data — a fictional service called *Aurora* with sample usage and configurations. Controls such as language, theme, Copy and the Configuration Explorer work; there is no real subscription behind them.

On each design's page you can switch the preview between mobile and desktop sizes, change its language and theme, or open it full-screen in a new tab.
